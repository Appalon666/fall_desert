### 1.1 Радкрыса (radrat) — Ржавый Пустырь

```

mutant wasteland rat, side view, facing LEFT, full body, for a 2D game.

=== CREATURE (identical in all 4 frames) ===
A dog-sized irradiated rat with a hunched back and a long naked tail.

large round EYE glowing hot RED with a bright white pinpoint highlight.
LIMBS: short muscular forelegs with long dirty claws, stronger hind legs.
TAIL: long, thick, hairless, segmented and scaly, dull pinkish-grey with a
darker tip, dragging in a lazy S-curve.
PALETTE: dark charcoal olive, rust brown, dull pinkish grey, acid green glow,
hot red eye. No purple, violet or magenta anywhere.
CONTRAST RULE (important): this creature is shown against a bright ORANGE SAND
background in the game, so keep the fur DARK and desaturated, and add a subtle
pale sand-coloured RIM LIGHT along the top of the back, head and tail so the
silhouette separates clearly from a warm bright background.

=== ART STYLE (identical in all 4 frames) ===
Hand-drawn 2D game art, cel-shaded, bold clean dark outline of even thickness

=== THE 4 FRAMES — a walk cycle (read left-to-right, top row first) ===
FRAME 1 (top-left) — CONTACT: front-left paw stretched forward and planted,
rear-right leg extended back, body at mid height, tail swung back.
FRAME 2 (top-right) — DOWN: weight over the front paws, body at its LOWEST,
back arched down, head dipped, tail low and trailing.
FRAME 3 (bottom-left) — PASSING: legs gathered under the body, body at mid
height moving forward, tail curling to the opposite side.
FRAME 4 (bottom-right) — UP: body at its HIGHEST, back arched up, hind legs
pushing off, front paws reaching forward, tail flicked up.

=== TECHNICAL (critical) ===
Background: a completely FLAT, UNIFORM, SOLID PURPLE CHROMA KEY, hex #A020F0,
filling the whole image behind and between all 4 frames. Pure chroma key purple
only — no gradient, no vignette, no texture, no pattern, no lighting falloff,
no grid lines, no frame borders, no numbers, no labels, no watermark.
Nothing purple, violet or magenta anywhere on the creature.
No shadow cast onto the background, no ground shadow, no ground line, no floor,
no scenery, no props.
No glow bloom, halo, spill or motion blur onto the purple — the green pustule
glow and red eye glow must stay tight against the body with a crisp outlined edge.
All 4 frames MUST use the exact same creature design, same colours, same
markings in the same places, same camera distance, same scale, and the paws must
sit on the exact same horizontal baseline in every frame.
Even margins around each frame, creature centred in its cell.
Square image, 2x2 layout, highest possible resolution.
```
